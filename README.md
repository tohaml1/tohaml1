# Anton Melanin

Finance and reconciliation analyst based in Limassol, Cyprus. I work on the gap between what a ledger says and what actually happened — matching records, investigating breaks, and explaining why two systems disagree.

**Background.** Corporate finance internships in digital health and oil and gas: accounts receivable reconciliation across 25+ counterparties, cost analysis across 12 cost centres during month-end close, variance investigation traced back to source documentation.

**Now.** Applying the same discipline to crypto and fiat payment flows. AML and transaction monitoring trained (Sumsub Academy, CPD-accredited). Working with on-chain data in Etherscan, Solscan, Arkham and Dune, and writing Python to reconcile it against off-chain records.

**Interested in** payment operations, settlements and reconciliation, and transaction monitoring roles.

### Projects

- **[onchain-reconciliation](https://github.com/tohaml1/onchain-reconciliation)** — matches on-chain transactions against an internal ledger, classifies breaks into seven root-cause types, outputs an investigation file and a reporting summary. Python, pandas, `Decimal` arithmetic.
- **[ofac-crypto-corpus](https://github.com/tohaml1/ofac-crypto-corpus)** — a counted dataset of the OFAC digital currency address list with four years of revision history, plus a screening script showing where asset-scoped matching quietly fails.
- **[foomcash-trace-report](https://github.com/tohaml1/foomcash-trace-report)** — on-chain trace of a February 2026 multi-chain incident: 40 transfer events recomputed, reported totals reconciled, cross-chain fund flow documented.
- **[break-triage](https://github.com/tohaml1/break-triage)** — a workflow for triaging reconciliation breaks: root-cause classification, gross and net exposure per category, and an escalation memo format.

### Stack

Python (pandas) · SQL · Excel · SAP ERP · Power BI · Etherscan / Solscan / Arkham / Dune
